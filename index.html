06 / BEFORE YOU COMMIT

Good questions.
Clear starting points.

Who owns the home during the programme?+

That depends on the agreed structure. The intended titleholder, financing obligations, and employee rights must be defined before launch. Living in a home does not itself confer ownership.

What happens if an employee leaves?+

The programme needs separate rules for voluntary departure, redundancy, and employer closure. These can address earned rewards, buyout options, occupancy, and the treatment of improvements.

Can we use our existing housing and LTI budget?+

The calculator compares that budget with estimated programme costs and shows whether additional funding is needed. It also shows the money required upfront.

Is financing guaranteed?+

No. Lending depends on the employer, property, proposed structure, and lender assessment. The rate in the calculator is an editable illustration, not a bank offer.

Can we start with a small pilot?+

Yes. Design a programme around a limited group of employees, agree the funding and participation terms, and learn from the pilot before a broader rollout.
